Subject: ClawHost Index Weekly #1: Hetzner fix, RackNerd back, 29 of 31 verified
Preview: What changed in this week's re-check, plus a cheap 8 GB pick.

Hi,

This is the first weekly email from ClawHost Index. The site went live on 16 October and the list is still small, so thanks for being an early reader.

On 19 October I checked every plan against the provider's own page. Here's what changed.

**Hetzner**
The CX22 is gone. Its replacement, the CX23 (2 vCPU, 4 GB, 40 GB NVMe), is about £5.05/mo (€5.99 ex VAT). My old listing used a third-party figure of about £2.62 ($3.49) from March. That was out of date. It's now fixed and verified. One catch: on 19 October Hetzner showed every cost-optimised plan, CX23 included, as "currently unavailable".

**RackNerd**
The 4 GB plan (about £3.75/mo, $59.99/yr, 3 vCPU, 60 GB) and the 8 GB plan (about £7.50/mo, $119.99/yr, 7 vCPU, 150 GB) are back on RackNerd's specials page, so both are verified. Both are paid yearly.

**Everything else**
Akamai (Linode) Shared 4 GB is confirmed on Akamai's own pricing page at about £18/mo ($24). UpCloud prices are unchanged, but their new Starter plans have replaced the old Developer plans. Prices didn't move at any of the other hosts I track.

29 of 31 plans are now verified, up from 25. Kamatera (a calculator estimate) and Oracle Cloud Always Free (specs not confirmed) are still unverified. No new plans this week. GBP figures use ECB rates from 16 October.

**Pick of the week**
Contabo Cloud VPS 4: 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB, about £3.71/mo (€4.40 ex VAT) for the first 24 months, with a one-click OpenClaw install. Treat this as a rough guide: 8 GB is what I'd go for if you want browser automation or to build Docker images on the server. It's an estimate, not a benchmark. The price is ex VAT, so UK buyers pay more, and check what it goes up to after the promo. If 4 GB is enough, the cheapest verified option is still OVHcloud VPS-1 at about £3.40/mo.

If a host or plan is missing or wrong, reply and tell me. The full table is at https://clawhostindex.com.
