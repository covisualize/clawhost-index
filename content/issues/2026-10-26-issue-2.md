**Kamatera**
Type A (4 vCPU, 4 GB, 50 GB) is now verified on Kamatera's own price calculator at $28/mo (about £20.95). The price hasn't changed, but before it came from a third-party tracker.

**Everything else**
Prices didn't move at any of the other hosts I track. Hetzner was checked separately this week, and the CX23 (about £5.02/mo, €5.99 ex VAT) still shows last week's 19 October check.

All 31 plans are now verified on the provider's own page, up from 29. No new plans and none dropped this week. GBP figures use ECB rates from 23 October.
